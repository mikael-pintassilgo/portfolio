# Mikhail Shchegolkov

## Product Analyst · Product Discovery · UX Research · Experimentation

I work at the intersection of **product analytics, user research and product design**, helping teams understand what users need, identify friction and make better product decisions.

Over the past 12+ years, I have worked on complex B2B software and ERP products, combining roles in **product analysis, UX research, interaction design and software development**.

This portfolio presents selected projects where I used research, data, experimentation and iterative design to reduce uncertainty and solve real product problems.

The cases range from **usability research and enterprise UX** to **A/B testing, competitive analysis and data-driven product discovery**.

I am particularly interested in complex products where understanding user behaviour and delivering real user value requires combining different perspectives rather than relying on a single discipline.
