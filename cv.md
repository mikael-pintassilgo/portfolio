# SELECTED PRODUCT ANALYTICS & DISCOVERY PROJECTS

## A/B Experiment — Evidence-based Product Decision

**Objective:** Determine whether replacing vertically stacked collapsible groups with horizontally arranged tabs would improve form usability.

- Defined task-completion time as the primary measure for the experiment.
- Analysed the experiment results using statistical confidence intervals.
- The confidence interval indicated that the new design did not provide the expected improvement.
- The existing interaction model was retained rather than introducing a change that would increase the learning cost for users.

**Skills demonstrated:** A/B Testing · Experiment Design · Statistics · Product Analytics · Decision Making
